" MARKAZ AFFILIATION "

CM Jeelani Dars is proudly affiliated with Markazu Saquafathi Sunniyya, a renowned institution known for its excellence in Islamic education and social service. Through this affiliation, we follow a well-structured curriculum that ensures quality education, discipline, and strong moral values for our students.

Students from our institution are eligible for direct admission to Markaz without an entrance interview, providing a smooth and assured pathway to higher Islamic studies. This opportunity helps students continue their education with confidence and ease.

CM JEELANI DARS

CM Jeelani Dars is affiliated with Markazu Saqafathi Sunniyya and provides a well-structured Islamic and academic learning program, combining modern education with spiritual development. Admissions are open for students from 6th standard and above.

Our Programs Include:

  • Islamic studies with moral and character education
  • Personality and leadership development
  • Higher Secondary (+1) streams – Science, Commerce, Humanities
  • Degree Programs – BSc, BBA, BCom, BA
  • Multi-language learning – English, Arabic, and more

Future Opportunities:

  • Assistance for continuing studies (Post Graduation)
  • Pathways to Al Azhar University
  • Admission support to Markazu Saqafathi Sunniyya

Facilities & Activities:

  • Skill development and academic support
  • Friendly and disciplined campus environment
  • Study trips (ziyara, industrial visit)
  • Sports and entertainment for overall growth

We focus on building strong faith, excellent character, and academic success, preparing students for both worldly education and a meaningful spiritual life.

CHISTHI THAHFEELUL QUR'AN

Chisthi Thahfeelul Qur'an, under CM Jeelani Dars, is a special program dedicated to teaching the Qur’an with correct pronunciation (Tajweed) and understanding.

Admissions are open for students from 5th standard onwards.

The program includes:

  • Basic to advanced Qur’an recitation
  • Tajweed and pronunciation training
  • Hifz (memorization) guidance
  • Spiritual and moral development
  • Individual care and guidance

Our aim is to help students recite the Qur’an correctly and grow with strong Islamic values.

=== SKILL ENHANCEMENT ===

COMPUTER SKILL ENHANCEMENT

At CM Jeelani Dars, we provide computer skill training to help students adapt to the modern world. Along with Islamic education, students learn basic computer knowledge, typing skills, and the use of essential software.

Our program helps students improve digital awareness, communication, and practical skills needed for studies and future careers.We aim to develop confident individuals who can balance both religious values and modern technology.

WRITING SKILL ENHANCEMENT

At CM Jeelani Dars, we focus on improving students’ writing skills to help them communicate clearly and confidently. Our program develops handwriting, grammar, vocabulary, and creative writing abilities. Students are guided in essay and paragraph writing, helping them express their ideas in a clear and effective way. We aim to build confident writers who can use their skills for both academic success and daily life.

SPEAKING SKILL ENHANCEMENT

At CM Jeelani Dars, we help students improve their speaking skills to communicate with confidence and clarity. Our program focuses on pronunciation, fluency, and effective expression. Students are trained through speeches, discussions, and presentations, helping them overcome fear and speak with confidence. We aim to develop strong communicators who can express their ideas clearly in both academic and daily life.

HADRATH SHADHULIYA GATHERING

At CM Jeelani Dars, we conduct a monthly Hadrath Shadhuliya gathering as a part of our spiritual activities. This program helps students and the community strengthen their connection with Allah through dhikr, du'a, and remembrance.

It is a time for spiritual reflection, unity, and renewal of faith. Through this gathering, we aim to create a peaceful environment that nurtures love for Islamic traditions and strengthens the spiritual well-being of everyone involved.
